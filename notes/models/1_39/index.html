    1_39¶

    Research note

    Part of the development diary of the single-agent work, kept for reference. Model names such as 1_38 are internal run ids (the published TMB and PMS policies are 1_76 and 1_77). File paths and commands refer to the private development repository, and numbers were measured with the evaluation setup of the time. The published design is under How it works; the final results are under Results.

    not adopted

    What changed: As 1_38, with the loss-of-separation penalty raised from 90 to 240 (sized to a stitched 40-flight training stream).

    Reproduce¶

    TADA_SEQUENCE_OBS=1 TADA_MAX_PICKS=2 python -u main_jax.py --env windowed --reward-mode outcome_pbrs --stitch-segments 2 --stitch-gap 120 900 --init-weights experiments/atc_run_1_36_windowed_multipick/final_model.zip --n-envs 32 --critic-warmup-steps 100000 --lr-max 3e-05 --final-lr 3e-06 --ent-coef 0.003 --warmup-frac 0.08 --vf-coef 0.25 --target-kl 0.05 --violation-penalty 240 --total-timesteps 5000000

# score: 20-flight and stitched 2x20, 10 attempts, lookahead (100 validation seeds)
TADA_SEQUENCE_OBS=1 TADA_MAX_PICKS=2 python analysis/score_windowed.py --models experiments/atc_run_1_39_windowed_safety/final_model.zip --seeds 100 --attempts 9
TADA_SEQUENCE_OBS=1 TADA_MAX_PICKS=2 python analysis/score_windowed.py --models experiments/atc_run_1_39_windowed_safety/final_model.zip --seeds 100 --segments 2 --gap 120 900
